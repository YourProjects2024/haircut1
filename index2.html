Meet Jessi Lynn Rushing

Jessi Lynn Rushing is an accomplished master barber, expert colorist, and non-surgical hair restoration certified specialist. Practicing her art at the highly sought-after J Danielle Salon & Color Studio, Jessi blends traditional Barbering precision with state-of-the-art modern styling methodologies.

Whether you are coming in for a fresh fade, an advanced multi-tone coloring session, customized premium volume extensions, or exploring the hair-restoring magic of Hairskeen, Jessi works with you one-on-one to create a tailor-made style that radiates authentic personal power and self-assurance.
