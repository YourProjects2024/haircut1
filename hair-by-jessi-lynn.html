What I Offer

Services tailored
to every client.

Whether you're after a clean taper, a bold colour transformation, or everything in between — there's something for everyone at the chair.

Men's Haircuts

Precision fades, tapers, and classic cuts that keep you looking sharp. Every cut finished with care and attention to detail.

Hot Shaves

Traditional hot towel straight razor shaves — the classic barbershop experience for the smoothest, closest shave around.
